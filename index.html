Studying Planetary Surfaces

Dora Elalaoui-Pinedo

Hello! I'm Dora, a senior undergraduate student at the University of Arizona double majoring in Planetary Geosciences and Data Science with an Applied Statistics Emphasis. I am interested in the composition and characterization of planetary surfaces across Mars, Earth, and Europa — including geomorphology, mineralogy, and the evolution of ice sheets and glaciers. I enjoy using imaging spectroscopy, remote sensing, field geology, and change detection techniques, along with data science and machine learning methods, to study active planetary surfaces and assess planetary habitability. I am also interested in planetary science mission instrumentation.

Research

Since 2023, I have conducted research spanning geomorphology, mineralogy, and stratigraphy, using a variety of datasets and tools.

Cataloging Strata in the South Polar Layered Deposits (SPLD) of Mars

University of Arizona, Lunar and Planetary Laboratory. August 2026 to present.

Undergraduate Student Researcher, advised by Dr. Shane Byrne.

I am producing HiRISE digital terrain models of the SPLD to catalog exposed strata using GIS. We hope to constrain the recent climate expressed in geomorphic and geological features.

Characterizing Changes in Morphology and Mineralogy of Interdunes at Olympia Undae, Mars

SETI Institute. June 2026 to August 2026.

REU Intern, advised by Dr. Janice Bishop and Dr. Lori Fenton.

I analyzed morphological and mineralogical patterns in Martian interdunes using CRISM and HiRISE data. I identified spectral evidence of iron sulfates in interdunes, including potential rozenite, contributing to the efforts to uncover aqueous processes shaping Mars's north polar climate history.

Enigmatic Polar Pits in the North Polar Layered Deposits of Mars: Mapping and Temporal Analysis

University of Arizona, Lunar and Planetary Laboratory. August 2023 to May 2025.

NASA Space Grant Intern, advised by Dr. Sarah Sutton.

I investigated meter-scale pits in the NPLD using HiRISE images. I located and mapped over 2,000 polar pits using QGIS and created digital terrain models to analyze potentially changing pit morphologies over time. Identifying evidence of active surface processes and potential faults in the NPLD advances our understanding of Mars's climate history.

Data Science Projects

Although predominately related to music, here are a few data science class projects I have worked on. These projects reflect the data pipeline, database design, and tool-building skills I bring to my planetary science research.

  • Music Analysis ETL

    I built a data pipeline (ETL) for a class project that combines Billboard Hot 100 charts, Spotify audio features, and Grammy nominations from 2015 to 2025 into one PostgreSQL database. It can answer questions like whether Grammy winners chart better and which audio features go with commercial success.

  • FoundinTranslation

    I led the creation of an R package with classmates that finds the lyrics of a requested song from Genius.com, cleans the lyrics, and translates them into over 100 languages, with a Shiny app for side-by-side comparison.
